### Opportunity cost and strongest alternative

The opening budget is 25, with four available new-operation slots. Spending 14 leaves 11 unspent; only 8 can carry forward, so 3 expire. No pending bonus is currently recorded. The selected missions can generate up to 5 pending resources if all three reward-bearing operations achieve their required verified activity; that is below the next bonus cap of 10. The potential bonus is not guaranteed.

The strongest alternative considered is to replace infiltration with a second research operation and a harvest operation elsewhere, retaining more progression and conditional resource income while avoiding a costly, uncertain political commitment. I prefer infiltration because it is the only selected operation with an explicit pact-and-base outcome that could advance a major strategic objective directly. This preference is qualitative: the packet supplies no calibrated conquest score or forecast that proves infiltration is worth more than compounding progression.

## 4. Outcomes that would change the next decision

1. Verified infiltration completion and its actual outcome: increase or decrease the priority given to further infiltration.
2. Verified productive harvest, abduction, and research rewards: revise the balance between resource accumulation and progression.
3. New surviving contacts or updated loss assignments with available search targets: reconsider regional tasking and whether to fund base searches.

A strategically meaningful probe of a specific player-base location is not available through research: research grants generic progression, while base search requires eligible search craft and only detects a nearby base under native range and chance rules. The only eligible base-search operation is in Southeast Asia, so there is no legal way in this portfolio to probe North Africa directly for a player base.
