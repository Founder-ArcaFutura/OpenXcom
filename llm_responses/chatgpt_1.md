## 2. Checkable justification

The portfolio diversifies immediate political pressure, conditional resource generation, persistent progression, and a possible long-term strategic foothold. It spends 14 of 25 resources and uses all four available operation slots.

- Research — North America (cost 2): Objective: complete research flights. Conditional benefit: +1 pending resource and +1 intelligence for a verified completed parent mission. Main uncertainty: interception and completion; regional placement does not answer a specific intelligence question. Intelligence is already 1, so the additional point has no demonstrated immediate gate benefit.
- Harvest — North Africa (cost 3): Objective: productive harvesting. Conditional benefit: +2 pending resources and +1 logistics. Main uncertainty: productive completion and survival. Four independent surviving-UFO reports support attention to North Africa, while five April loss assignments indicate operational exposure, not a measured failure rate. Logistics would rise from 1 to 2 if rewarded.
- Abduction — Southeast Asia (cost 3): Objective: productive abduction. Conditional benefit: +2 pending resources and +1 adaptation. Main uncertainty: productive completion. An April surviving-UFO contact and three April loss assignments support continued attention, but do not establish a base or mission failure. Adaptation is already 2; no immediate additional gate is established.
- Infiltration — Europe (cost 6): Objective: seek a country pact and alien base through mission completion. Conditional benefit: those outcomes may advance conquest, but neither is guaranteed and neither currently produces recurring resources. Main uncertainty: country vulnerability, duration, completion, and success probability are unknown. Europe has older surviving-UFO evidence and five April loss assignments, but these do not establish infiltration suitability.

### Opportunity cost and strongest alternative

The 11 retained resources are below the carry cap of 8 only after applying the cap: at most 8 carry forward, so 3 expire if unspent. With next allowance 20 and next bonus cap 10, the next budget is 28 before any eligible pending rewards, assuming no other changes. The selected portfolio's potential 4 resource reward is conditional, not guaranteed.

The strongest alternative is two harvests, one abduction, and one infiltration, costing 15. It could yield more resources and logistics, but sacrifices research's intelligence progression and still cannot guarantee productive activity. I prefer the selected portfolio because it diversifies progression while preserving funds for the next decision. The absence of a calibrated conquest score means this preference is judgment, not a proven optimum.

## 3. Known, inferred, and unknown

- Known: Opening resources are 25; intelligence is 1, logistics 1, adaptation 2; four slots are available. Infiltration is gated at month 2 with adaptation at least 2 and intelligence at least 1. The supplied eligible list permits all four selections.
- Inferred: North Africa and Southeast Asia merit continued attention because admitted reports overlap loss-assignment clues. Europe is a plausible infiltration allocation, not a demonstrated best target. Existing Southeast Asia harvest and European/North African search operations continue without new funding.
- Unknown: Base locations, country vulnerability, mission success probabilities, actual future rewards, and the relative strategic value of political pressure versus resources. No valid numerical conquest metric can resolve these tradeoffs.
