# Offline conquest incentive probe, preregistered conditions

Five historical decision states: February through June 1. February is the legacy supplemental January report packet; the remaining four are saved native portfolio receipts. Same fixed checkpoint, cash, costs, prerequisites, pending operations, evidence and legal targets within each pairing. All arms apply the same current static infiltration geography correction. Current shared v9 planner is used for every baseline, not historical v7/v8 binaries.

Arms: baseline live wording; explicit_current conditional native reward descriptions; hypothetical conditional reward descriptions. Each is tested in normal and reversed PLAN option order. TARGET order is unchanged. No extra strategy chooser, advisor, quota, fine-tuning, native rebuild or live change. Explicit arms add the same concise uncertainty/payout-limit sentence to PLAN state; TARGET packets are unchanged. Explicit current also states harvest's existing logistics-to-base gate. Hypothetical uses that same format.

Hypothetical proposal: research pays no resources and adds intelligence only below a cap of two (existing intelligence is not removed). Harvest/abduction pay three resources instead of two, with existing progression. Successfully completed terror/surface attacks pay six resources and two local influence units; defeated operations pay zero. Site creation alone does not qualify. Search still does not assault or pay resources. Existing base economics and infiltration possibilities remain unchanged. All mission-completion cash remains next-month and capped. Terror outcome admission and non-base local influence storage/effects would need native implementation; neither is represented as live. Influence does not secure an automatic pact. No success probabilities, expected-value estimates or universal reward scalar are invented.

This is a prompt sensitivity experiment about proposed incentives, not a changed reward-trained policy or simulation. Choices may reflect words, numbers, labels or order rather than strategic reasoning. Replaying the same deterministic checkpoint is not a set of independent samples. Five recorded monthly states are from one campaign and do not form an alternate trajectory: historical rewards are not recomputed under the proposal. More terror is not itself a quality metric.

Report legal validity, mission mix, spend, order agreement, prerequisite preparation, unavailable actions, token-limit rejections, and June specifically. Preserve all failures. Do not silently shorten prompts or increase model limits if a treatment fails. Do not promote the treatment to live based solely on this probe.
