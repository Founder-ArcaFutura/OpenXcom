# Preregistered 2^4 conquest incentive factorial

Factors (bit order C,S,P,T): remove research resource payout; cap research intelligence gains at two; raise harvest/abduction payout from two to three; reward successful terror/surface attack with six resources and two local influence units. Off values are the live mechanics. Existing intelligence above two is retained; saturation limits future research gains, not base progression. Site creation does not qualify as terror success. All cash remains conditional, next-month and capped. Proposed terror admission and non-base influence effects are not implemented live.

All sixteen combinations use matched explicit reward descriptions and the same conditional-payout limitation. Five fixed historical decision states, February–June, each with canonical, reversed and fixed seeded PLAN order: 240 factorial portfolios. An additional 15 unmodified live-wording controls separate the description treatment from reward factors. Targets retain their original order, a limitation. The seeded permutation is reproducible from the available action IDs and fixed experiment tag, identical across conditions sharing action IDs. Partial portfolios can change available IDs as affordability changes.

The model is the same fixed career-trained checkpoint. CPU-only torch 2.10.0+cpu is installed despite the AMD RX 9060 XT hardware. No new runtime, API, training, native build or game execution. Exact ordered-packet caching uses state, ordered action IDs/text, and instructions. Previously observed identical packets can be reused only with matching checkpoint and live model/planner hashes. Cache hits are reuse, not independent inference. New inference/cache counts and provenance are reported separately.

Outcomes: legality, input-limit failures, spend, total deferral, mission counts, exact and mission-mix agreement across orders. Main effects are paired on/off differences averaged equally over the other three factors, months and orders; interactions use differences of differences with the same matched weighting. No significance tests or claims of independent samples. Neither more terror, spend nor mix is inherently better. First report all outcomes; do not select a condition solely for making terror happen. Preserve failures and do not silently shorten prompts.

Each month is a separate probe of an observed state. Rewards from an alternate February are not propagated into March–June. No success odds, expected campaign values, political simulation or actual mission outcomes are inferred. All five states lack logistics for direct base construction; funded assault remains unavailable. This probes reward-description sensitivity and implementation candidates, not game balance itself. Original saves and the live v9 policy remain unchanged.
